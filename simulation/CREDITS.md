# Clip credits

The clips in motion-clips are motion-triggered trail camera recordings from the Hillsborough County, Florida ELAPP wildlife monitoring program, published by James Robe on the Internet Archive under the Creative Commons Public Domain Mark 1.0. Each was trimmed to 15 seconds and re-encoded without audio. Some show animals and some show people.
